Deploy the OAA Launchpad on Base

Your wallet deploys the contract and signs every step. Nothing is sent without your confirmation in the wallet. Keep about 0.002 ETH on Base for the network fees.

2bKeep launches closed until $OAA is live

Pause right after the deploy: nobody can launch a token while paused (trading of existing tokens is never affected). Unpause on launch day. Only the owner can do this; do it before step 3.

Statusunknown

2cSet the $OAA buyback pool (on launch day, after the $OAA pool exists)

Tells the launchpad which Uniswap V4 pool to use: 0.3% of every buy (paid in ETH) buys $OAA there and sends it to the burn address. Until this is set the ETH waits safely in the contract and is spent later. The pool must have no hooks. Only the owner, so do it before step 3.

3Hand the admin rights to the Treasury Safe (on launch day, after opening)

Starts the 2-step ownership transfer to the OAA Treasury Safe. Afterwards the Safe owners confirm acceptOwnership() in the Safe app. The owner can only pause new launches, and change the treasury address; liquidity and fees can never be touched.

L1Deploy the $OAA launch hook (before launch day)

The hook guards the $OAA pool on Uniswap V4: in the first 10 minutes the fee falls from 25% to 1%; in the first 5 minutes a single buy can be at most 20,000,000 $OAA and each wallet can buy once per block (no limit on the total). From minute 10 everything is at 1%. The connected wallet becomes the only wallet that can launch, so connect the wallet that holds the $OAA. Compiled with solc.

L2Verify the hook

Publishes the exact source code of the hook on Sourcify, so everyone can read the anti-sniper rules.

L3Launch $OAA (launch day)

One transaction creates the $OAA / ETH pool, puts your $OAA in as one-sided liquidity in three ranges (no ETH needed) and makes your first buy at the normal 1% fee. Nobody can trade in that block. The liquidity is locked in the hook forever; its trading fees go to the Treasury Safe.

2cSet the $OAA buyback pool (on launch day, after the $OAA pool exists)

Tells the launchpad which Uniswap V4 pool to use: 0.3% of every buy (paid in ETH) buys $OAA there and sends it to the burn address. Until this is set the ETH waits safely in the contract and is spent later. For the pool from step L3 use fee 8388608 (dynamic), tick spacing 200 and the hook address; they are filled in after the launch. Only the owner, so do it before step 3.

A1Deploy the OAA Agent Registry (on-chain agents)

Anyone can register an SI agent; clients pay per job in ETH or USDC, the payment waits in the contract until the agent delivers, then 80% goes to the agent owner and 20% to the Treasury Safe (100% for OAA's own agents). The deploying wallet is the admin (verify / suspend agents, cannot move funds); hand it to the Safe later. Compiled with solc.

A2Verify the registry

3Hand the admin rights to the Treasury Safe (on launch day, after opening)

Starts the 2-step ownership transfer to the OAA Treasury Safe. Afterwards the Safe owners confirm acceptOwnership() in the Safe app. The owner can only pause new launches, and change the treasury address; liquidity and fees can never be touched.
